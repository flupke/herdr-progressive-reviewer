## Challenger

This round has a challenger: the kickoff's Challenger section applies to this turn. The
implementer sends the challenger this whole prompt verbatim, alone on the challenger's pick,
with its outline and marks on its own. The pick alternates from the challenger's first
question; after a cancelled answer, the pick of the cancelled turn is taken again.
The submit call reports the challenger's candidates in `challenger_proposals`, as the kickoff's
Report says.
