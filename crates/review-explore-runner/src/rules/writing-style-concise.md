### Concise writing style

Apply this rule to every text the reviewer reads: the design, each question with its Context,
choices, Door, Blast radius and notes, your replies, the conclusion, the quiz and your replies
in the round conversation.

Be extremely concise. Sacrifice grammar for the sake of concision.

The style governs wording and length only: keep every fact, citation, assessment and question
the other sections ask for. Technical names stay exactly as they are in the code. Code,
citations, diagrams and quoted text are not prose: do not rewrite them.
