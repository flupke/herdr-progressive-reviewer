Question 2One-way doorBlind pick

What should happen to the replies still waiting in the queue when the reviewer closes the pane?

When the reviewer closes the pane, some replies may still wait in the queue: the reviewer wrote them less than two seconds ago. Each one is already saved in its thread; what is at stake is whether the agent hears about them.

The change sends them in one last notification, with the reason Closing, before the pane saves its threads.

Choices

Drop the waiting notifications, and show how many were dropped as the pane closes
