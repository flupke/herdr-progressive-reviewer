flowchart) or a state before and after (a state diagram); a comparison is a table. Keep labels
short and use at most about 12 nodes, with rounded corners, as in `queue("Reply queue")`. Class
the components the change adds `new` and the ones it changes `changed`, as in

it at most five participants. To avoid parse errors, quote every label with punctuation, as in
`A["Reply queue (old)"]`; put no `;` in a sequence message; and write no HTML, `click`, `style`,
`classDef` or `%%{init}%%`. A diagram that does not parse shows as its source.
