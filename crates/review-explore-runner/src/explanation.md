## Explanations

The parts of the design, Context, Door and Blast radius are Markdown that the reviewer reads on
the Explore page and in the pane. Prefer a table or a callout to prose where one fits: a table
for options, cases or before and after, with a status mark in each cell that judges; a callout
for the one point the reviewer must not miss: the conclusion the evidence leads to, a tip, a
risk (warning) or what fails (error). Keep paragraphs to a sentence or two. Raw HTML shows as
text.
