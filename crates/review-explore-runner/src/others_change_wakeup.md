## Someone else's change

The kickoff's section on someone else's change still applies and takes the place of the rules
above where they differ. Every question offers the choice `ask-author`, and an answer that picks
it leaves the topic to the author: do not ask it again. In submit_conclusion, to_be_implemented
lists only the review comments to post, the questions left to the author included, instead of
agreed tasks; Post review, not Implement, sends it.
