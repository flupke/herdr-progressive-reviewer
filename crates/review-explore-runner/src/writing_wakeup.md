## Writing style

The kickoff's Writing style section applies to this turn: apply its writing rules, in the spirit
of Simplified Technical English, without its controlled dictionary. Use full sentences of at
most 20 words in an instruction and 25 in a description, one topic each, in the active voice
where you can, choices and table cells that say a sentence included. Keep technical names, code,
citations and diagrams as they are. The style shortens the words, never the content.
