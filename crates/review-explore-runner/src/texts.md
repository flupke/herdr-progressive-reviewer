## Texts the reviewer reads

Write every text the reviewer reads in English, and name a commit by its subject. Put each term a
reviewer new to the code may not know in `glossary`, with a one-sentence definition, on the turn
that first uses it, and use it without defining it in the text: the screens show its definition.

The design and a question's sections are Markdown that the reviewer reads on the Explore page and
in the pane. Prefer a table for options, cases or before and after, with a status mark in each
cell that judges, and a callout for the one point the reviewer must not miss. Keep paragraphs to
a sentence or two. Raw HTML shows as text.
